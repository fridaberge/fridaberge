# Hi 👋 I'm Frida Berge

I’m a graduate of the University of Oslo with a bachelor’s degree in **Informatics: Robotics and Intelligent Systems**. I also spent an exchange semester at City University of Hong Kong.

My interests span **robotics**, **embedded systems**, and **software development, machine learning and AI**. I enjoy solving problems through programming and algorithms, as well as exploring how software interacts with physical hardware.

Through university projects, I’ve worked on robotic control, FPGA/SoC development, algorithms and machine learning. As a robotics teaching assistant, I also gained experience explaining technical concepts and guiding students through programming exercises.

## Areas of Interest

- **Robotics:** modelling, simulation and controlling physical robots.
- **Embedded Systems:** hardware–software integration, digital logic, sensors and motor control.
- **Software, ML & AI:** programming, algorithms, data structures, and implementing and evaluating learning models.
